# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Cards:
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Orders
- Units Sold

Visuals:
- Monthly revenue/profit trend
- Revenue and profit by region
- Profit by category

Filters:
- Date
- Region
- Category
- Customer Segment

## Page 2 — Product & Customer Analysis
Visuals:
- Top products by profit
- Revenue by customer segment
- Profit margin by category
- Quantity by product

## Page 3 — Pricing & Discount Analysis
Visuals:
- Discount level vs average profit
- Discount level vs margin
- Revenue/profit relationship

## Page 4 — Management Recommendations
Document:
1. Underperforming regions/categories
2. High-margin products
3. Discounting patterns
4. Suggested actions
5. Data limitations

Use actual calculated findings from the dataset; never invent business outcomes.
